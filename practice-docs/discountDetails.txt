One more important thing (Best Practice)
Never calculate discounts in the Cart or Order.
Only calculate the discount when the product is created or updated, then save the result in salePrice.

Your flow should be:

Admin
   ↓
Create/Update Product
   ↓
Calculate salePrice
   ↓
Save in MongoDB
   ↓
Frontend reads salePrice
   ↓
Cart uses salePrice
   ↓
Order uses salePrice
   ↓
Payment uses salePrice

This keeps the cart, checkout, and payment logic simple and avoids recalculating discounts repeatedly.

Literature on Discount Price on Ecommerce platforms:

Ecommerce websites use various price discounts to drive sales, clear inventory, and boost average order value. 

The most effective types include percentage-off (classic X% off), fixed cash (dollar-off), BOGO deals, free shipping thresholds, and bundled pricing.

Strategic discounting influences buyer psychology and improves conversion rates. The most common types of price promotions used by online retailers include:

1. Direct & Quantitative Reductions

   i. Percentage Discounts: The most common approach (e.g., "20% off sitewide"). Often preferred by shoppers for smaller or moderately priced items.
    
    ii. Fixed Amount / Cash Discounts: Flat reductions (e.g., "$10 off orders over $75"). This works exceptionally well for higher-priced items as it feels more tangible.

2. Value-Added & Grouped Offers

    i. Bundle Discounts: Grouping complementary products together for a lower overall price than purchasing them individually. This boosts the Average Order Value (AOV).

    ii. BOGO (Buy One Get One) / Multi-Tiered Promotions: Rewards customers for buying larger quantities (e.g., "Buy 1 Get 1 50% off" or "3 for 2"). Excellent for clearing out excess stock.
    
    iii. Free Shipping Thresholds: Removing shipping fees when a user reaches a specific cart total.

3. Exclusivity & Behavioral Triggers

    i. Coupon Codes / Promo Codes: Alphanumeric codes entered at checkout. Ideal for rewarding email subscribers or applying influencer campaigns.
    
    ii. Loyalty & Membership Discounts: Ecosystem-style perks (e.g., early access, member-only deals) utilized by services like Amazon Prime to build repeat business.
    
    iii. First-Time Buyer / Abandoned Cart Offers: Tailored, targeted discounts sent to new visitors (e.g., pop-ups) or users who left items in their digital carts.

4. Time-Sensitive Tactics

    i. Flash Sales & Countdown Timers: Deep, short-term discounts (e.g., "50% off for the next 2 hours"). These create natural urgency.
    
    ii. Seasonal & Event Sales: Promotions timed around holidays like Black Friday or regional festivals to capture seasonal spending spikes.

Understanding which discount fits your specific audience can help maximize sales without heavily diluting your brand's value...
